Illicit Activities

The Star Club

Madam Star's discreet establishment sits behind a respectable club front. Businessmen, bookmakers, officials and policemen sometimes cross paths here.

Madam Star:

The Star Club is available during both phases, but the crowd changes with the hour. Day and night use different encounter pools. The factory continues operating only during the Business Day.

The Star Club itself is well-connected and rarely raided. A major vice raid is roughly a twice-a-year danger, not a nightly trap.

Backroom Poker

The Neighborhood Game is open by default. Meeting Eddie Doyle through Madam Star gets you into the Commerce Club. Playing Commerce Club games can put you in Harrison Vale's orbit; Vale is the path to an Embassy Room invitation.

Neighborhood Game

Workers, drivers, clerks and local regulars. Low stakes and open access.

Commerce Club

Shopkeepers, salesmen, contractors and professionals. Eddie Doyle introduction required.

Embassy Room

Industrialists, attorneys, officials and wealthy guests. Harrison Vale invitation required.
