The Star Club

Madam Star's discreet establishment sits behind a respectable club front. Businessmen, bookmakers, officials and policemen sometimes cross paths here.

Madam Star:

The Star Club itself is well-connected and rarely raided. A major vice raid is roughly a twice-a-year danger, not a nightly trap.

Backroom Poker

Private games exist across Washington. Each circle has different stakes, patrons, information and police attention.

Neighborhood Game

Workers, drivers, clerks and local regulars. Low stakes, easier introductions.

Commercial Game

Shopkeepers, salesmen, contractors and professionals. More money and better business gossip.

Private Society Game

Industrialists, attorneys, officials and wealthy guests. Serious stakes. Introduction required.
