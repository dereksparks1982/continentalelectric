# Federal Electric — Fight World Design

## Purpose

The fight world gives Federal Electric a recurring legal-sports and underground-social layer without replacing the company-management core.

Time is the pressure mechanism. A sporting event or private invitation may happen while the player also has factory work, contracts, meetings, racing, or other opportunities competing for the same phase.

## Daily structure

### Business Day
- Federal Electric operations are active
- Federal Exchange is open, with opening quotations visible and intraday movement hidden
- Capital Race Grounds is the active Legal Sports venue
- private prizefights may occasionally be scheduled during the day
- Madam Star uses her daytime encounter pool

### Washington Night
- Federal Electric's ordinary business controls are closed
- Federal Exchange is closed and final quotations are posted
- licensed boxing becomes the active Legal Sports venue
- private prizefights may also occur at night
- cockfighting is currently a nighttime underground activity
- Madam Star uses her evening encounter pool

## Legal Sports

### Capital Race Grounds
Implemented foundation:
- persistent horses
- four qualifying heats
- two semifinals
- Main Event
- automatic schedule on the authoritative Business Day clock
- advance Win / Place / Show betting on any field already established
- automatic wager settlement while the player is elsewhere
- Race & Wager Ledger
- chance to meet Eddie Doyle among the bettors

There is no arbitrary game-imposed wager ceiling. Future practical limits or pool effects require historically researched rules.

### Licensed boxing
Implemented foundation:
- active during Washington Night
- persistent fighter roster
- weight classes
- fighting styles
- age
- records and knockouts
- condition, confidence, injuries and medical status
- scheduled four-bout card
- opening bout, featured bout, co-main event and main event
- automatic resolution on the Washington Night clock
- winner betting
- no arbitrary game-imposed wager ceiling
- Commission Fighter Registry
- fight and wager ledger
- decision score totals
- chance to meet fight-world contacts while attending
- major results feed the next morning's Metropolitan Ledger Sports section

The current fight simulation uses ratings, style interaction, stamina, power, defense, chin, condition and uncertainty. It is a gameplay foundation, not yet a claim that every rule exactly reproduces a 1938 commission bout.

## Illicit Activities

The player-facing category is **Illicit Activities**.

Current/foundation systems:
- Backroom Poker
- Underground Leads
- Private Prizefights
- Cockfighting
- recurring bookmaker contact
- private fight promoter contact
- phase-aware Madam Star encounters

### Private Prizefights
- independently unlockable
- may be scheduled during either Business Day or Washington Night
- deliberately capable of competing with legitimate work and legal sports for the player's time
- persistent underground fighters
- betting
- automatic resolution on the authoritative phase clock
- Underground Ledger

### Cockfighting
- independently unlockable
- currently scheduled during Washington Night
- persistent named entries
- betting
- automatic result resolution
- Underground Ledger

Results remain private information unless a later public event, raid, scandal, or newspaper-worthy consequence makes them public.

## Discovery network

Underground content does not use one rigid quest chain.

Possible discovery paths currently include:
- Eddie Doyle, bookmaker
- Capital Race Grounds
- licensed boxing contacts
- Marty Kane, fight promoter
- Madam Star

Future paths may include poker contacts and additional Washington NPCs.

Knowing one underground circle does not automatically reveal every other circle.

## Eddie Doyle

Eddie Doyle is a recurring information source rather than a disposable unlock key.

The player can ask what is running. Depending on circumstances, Eddie may:
- reveal a private prizefight
- reveal a cockfight
- confirm the schedule of an already known event
- have no useful information

Chasing an underworld lead consumes world time.

## Madam Star

Madam Star is available in both active phases.

Business Day:
- Brief Visit
- Afternoon Visit
- quieter daytime encounter pool

Washington Night:
- Brief Evening Visit
- Evening Visit
- busier evening encounter pool

The same establishment therefore exposes different parts of Washington depending on the hour.

## Betting principle

Federal Electric does not impose arbitrary videogame ceilings merely to keep bets small.

A wager is limited by:
- the player's available personal cash
- whether betting is still open
- any later historically researched or in-world restriction that has a real reason to exist

The same principle applies to stock purchases.

## Newspaper and Daily Report

The private Daily Report appears after Washington Night.

It may include:
- company revenue and expenses
- opening/closing personal and company cash
- stock trading results
- horse-racing wager net
- licensed-boxing wager net
- underground wager net
- notable private events

The Metropolitan Ledger is different. It appears the next morning and reports public information. Major horse-racing and licensed-boxing results currently feed its Sports section.

## Historical research still required

Before deeper systems are labeled historically authentic, verify:
- 1938 District of Columbia boxing commission procedure and rules
- Washington-area boxing venues, card timing, purses, judging and medical suspensions
- legal status and practical handling of boxing wagering
- race betting mechanics, takeout, pools, minimums and practical limits
- cockfighting legality/enforcement in D.C., Maryland and Virginia
- terminology and enforcement surrounding private prizefighting and bookmaking

Until verified, specific fighters, promoters, bookmakers, venues and underground events remain fictional simulation content.
