# Design Decisions

This file records decisions that should not drift silently during development.

## 2026-10-01

- Title: **Continental Electric**.
- Browser-based management/life simulation.
- Work on **main** only unless Derek explicitly authorizes another branch.
- Fictional American electrical/industrial company inspired by the breadth of major historical manufacturers, particularly the general industrial shape associated with General Electric, without using GE as the playable corporation.
- Setting begins in the late 1930s and runs into World War II, with later postwar play planned.
- Company management is the primary game. The owner's personal life is a parallel simulation.
- UI direction: **1930s/1940s corporate spreadsheet and paperwork**, not a conventional colorful tycoon dashboard.
- Separate corporate and personal finances.
- Functional stock market required.
- Stock universe is period appropriate. Prefer fictional corporations grounded in real historical sectors and market conditions.
- Newspapers are a core information system and should reflect real historical events for the current game date.
- Relevant Georgia Sun systems are conceptual donors: time/turns, ledger, newspapers, event choices, reputation, NPC memory, poker, horse racing, investments, debt, legal consequences, family/legacy, and long-save history.
- Poker and horse racing should be ported conceptually and improved for the new period and setting.
- Player can pursue respectable, ruthless, corrupt, reckless, or self-destructive strategies.
- Product quality/durability versus cost/profit is a core decision system.
- Wartime government contracts and industrial conversion are core progression.
- Fraud, corruption, contract violations, and other crimes can lead to investigations, financial ruin, loss of contracts, and prison.
- No universal morality meter. Consequences come through systems and people.
- New York is the leading setting direction: New York City for finance/society and an upstate industrial works for manufacturing. Final fictional city/factory location is not yet locked.
