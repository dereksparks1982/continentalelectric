# Federal Electric — Design Decisions

This file records decisions that should not drift silently during development.

## 2026-10-01

- Title changed from **Continental Electric** to **Federal Electric**.
- Repository: `dereksparks1982/federalelectric`.
- Browser-based historical industrial, financial, political, and life-management simulation.
- Work on **main** only unless Derek explicitly authorizes another branch.
- Federal Electric is the permanent center of the game. Finance, society, corruption, war, and politics orbit the electrical company rather than replacing it.
- Setting begins in **1938**, before direct U.S. entry into World War II.
- Washington, D.C. is the leading headquarters/political setting. Additional industrial locations may be added as the company expands.
- UI direction: **1930s/1940s corporate spreadsheet and paperwork**, not a conventional colorful tycoon dashboard.
- Separate corporate and personal finances.
- Functional stock market is the current build priority.
- Federal Electric begins **privately held**.
- An IPO is optional. The player may keep Federal Electric private forever.
- If Federal Electric later becomes public, corporate treasury, personal cash, and the player's FE shares remain separate.
- Stock universe uses fictional period-style corporations grounded in real historical sectors and broad market conditions.
- Fictional names may evoke historical industrial archetypes, but historical facts and market conditions should be researched independently.
- Newspapers are a core information system and should reflect real historical events for the current game date.
- Verified public-domain historical newspapers may be preserved in a separate archive with provenance. Fictional in-game reporting must remain distinguishable from reproduced historical documents.
- Company management remains primary. The owner's personal life is a parallel simulation.
- Player may pursue respectable, ruthless, corrupt, reckless, or self-destructive strategies.
- Dirty-profit choices can exist as abstract strategic decisions with systemic consequences; the game should not become a practical manual for tax evasion or defeating investigations.
- Product quality/durability versus cost/profit is a core decision system.
- Wartime government contracts and industrial conversion are core progression.
- Federal Electric should eventually produce historically plausible electrical and aviation-related components when research supports each product.
- Financial irregularities can accumulate exposure; postwar tax/audit events are possible but not guaranteed.
- Tax/accounting systems should be strategically meaningful without technical tax-law micromanagement.
- Fraud, corruption, contract violations, and other crimes can lead to investigations, financial ruin, loss of contracts, and prison.
- No universal morality meter. Consequences come through systems and people.
- A later optional political career can grow from the player's accumulated business, press, government, social, and scandal history, potentially extending toward a presidential campaign.
- Core gameplay expansion is planned as free updates to the same game rather than paid gameplay DLC.
